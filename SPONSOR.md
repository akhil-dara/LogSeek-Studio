# Support LogSeek Studio

LogSeek Studio is free and open source, and it will stay that way.

If it saves you time and you would like to support its development, there are two ways:

- [Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/akhil-dara)
- [Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/akhildara1l)

Support helps cover the time spent on fixes, new file formats and documentation.

Other ways to help cost nothing:

- Report a file that is read wrongly, with a few invented lines that show the problem, in [Issues](https://github.com/akhil-dara/LogSeek-Studio/issues).
- Suggest what would make your own work easier.
- Tell a colleague who reads large logs.

Thank you.
